Final-year medical student at Siriraj Hospital working toward an academic career in surgery. A self-directed learner who advances fastest under strong mentorship, I seek responsibility beyond my stage of training and treat setbacks as recalibration rather than retreat. My subspecialty choice remains open between cardio-vascular-thoracic and hepato-pancreato-biliary surgery — the latter the field of a research question I conceived — and I intend to settle it through sustained clinical exposure during the service period.
